# FEAT-31: Weekly progress email for parents (Plus)

> Implements: WEM-01..06 (D26; plan row FEAT-31 in `05-phase3-plan.md`, §4 "weekly email" is Plus) · Uses: ENT-01 (`activeEntitlement`, FEAT-29), the synced `children`, `rounds`, `attempts`, `badges`, `daily_usage` (FEAT-14/25), mail through Resend (`mail.pb.js`, FEAT-13), SCR-13 (account), SCR-20 (Plus list) · Screens: SCR-13 gains the "Weekly progress email" switch
> Status: built (T1–T5), to verify · Branch: `feat/FEAT-31-weekly-email` · 2026-11-20 (auto mode: decisions below taken by Claude on Ken's standing instruction)

## Scope

A signed-in Plus parent turns on a weekly email on SCR-13. Every Monday morning (family's own time) the server sends one email to the account's address summarising last Monday to Sunday for each child: time practised, rounds, questions and accuracy (with the week before), up to three topics to practise, new badges. It reads only records the app already syncs; nothing new is collected from the child. Every email has a one-click "turn off" link.

Not in scope: daily or monthly emails, emails to more than one address, charts or images in the email, AI analysis, push notifications, a web settings page, translations (English only, D-scope).

## Design decisions (this feature)

Auto mode (Ken, 2026-11-20): each takes the recommended option; the reason follows the dash.

- **WE-1 One email per family, a section per child.** — The plan says "per child"; with siblings (D20) that would be up to four emails each Monday. One email with a section per child (in `children` creation order) says the same with less inbox noise. A child with no activity gets one line, "No practice this week." No email at all when no child practised (no time, no rounds) that week. — A nag about nothing is not a report.
- **WE-2 Off by default, opt-in on SCR-13.** Fields on `users`: `weekly_email` (bool, default false) and `weekly_email_tz` (number, minutes east of UTC, −720..840, default 480 = Singapore). The parent's own record is already writable by its owner (`users.updateRule`). — The email carries the child's nickname and results; sending it needs the parent to ask (PDPA consent, NFR-14). Off also keeps free-tier and old-app accounts silent.
- **WE-3 Who gets it.** An account gets the email when `weekly_email` is true, `verified` is true and Plus is active at send time (`activeEntitlement`, FEAT-29). Losing Plus stops the email and keeps the setting, so it comes back with Plus (same spirit as PD-6).
- **WE-4 Time zone from the device.** When the switch is turned on, and whenever SCR-13 opens with the switch on and the device's offset differs, the app writes `weekly_email_tz = DateTime.now().timeZoneOffset.inMinutes`. The week is Monday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 at that offset. — PocketBase's JS VM has no time-zone database; a fixed offset is exact for Singapore (no DST) and close enough elsewhere, and it refreshes whenever the parent opens SCR-13.
- **WE-5 Hourly job, Monday 08:00 local, catch-up, once per week.** Cron `weekly_email` runs at minute 7 of every hour. For each candidate (WE-3) whose local time is between Monday 08:00 and Wednesday 08:00, and who has no `weekly_emails` row for that week, it builds the email, sends it and writes the row. A send that throws writes nothing, so the next hour retries; after Wednesday 08:00 that week is dropped. At most 200 sends per run (the rest go next hour). — Survives a server restart or a Resend outage on Monday without ever sending twice. Minute 7 keeps it off the 01:00 crash digest and the backup.
- **WE-6 Collection `weekly_emails`** (superuser only, every rule `null`): `user` → users (cascade delete), `week` (text `yyyy-MM-dd`, the local Monday the email covers), `status` (`sent` / `skipped`), `children` (number of child sections), autodate `created`; unique index `(user, week)`. `skipped` = nothing practised (WE-1). Rows older than 180 days are deleted by the job. — The send log is also what Ken checks in the admin.
- **WE-7 What a child's section holds** (all in the family's week, WE-4):
  - *Time*: sum of `daily_usage.seconds_used` (all devices) for `day` in the seven local days, shown as "1 h 12 min on 5 days" (days with > 0 s). Under a minute shows "less than a minute".
  - *Rounds*: `rounds` with `finished_at` in the week; "14 rounds (2 revision)"; average stars skipped.
  - *Questions*: `attempts` with `answered_at` in the week: count and % correct, "132 questions, 86% right". When the week before had ≥ 10 attempts, add "(80% the week before)".
  - *Topics to practise*: topic = the first two dot parts of `question_id` (`p1.money.coins.004` → `p1.money`; works for mixed revision rounds, whose `topic_id` is `mixed`). Topics with ≥ 5 attempts this week and accuracy < 80%, lowest first, at most 3: "Money (62%), Time (68%)". None: "No weak spots this week." — Weekly numbers are small, so the thresholds are lower than PAR-07's 10 attempts and 70%; 80% matches the app's "passed" feel without listing everything.
  - *Badges*: `badges` with `earned_at` in the week, by name: "New badges: Perfect 10, 3-Day Streak".
  - Times in the data are UTC ISO strings as synced; the job compares them with the week's UTC bounds.
- **WE-8 Names on the server.** `server/pb_hooks/lib/names.js` holds the topic id → name and badge id → name maps, copied from `lib/domain/topics.dart` and `lib/domain/badges.dart`. App test `test/tool/server_names_test.dart` fails when the two differ, so a new topic or badge must update both. An unknown id is shown as its id. — The server can't read Dart; reading names out of the bank JSON would miss badges and needs Plus-protected file access.
- **WE-9 Email shape.** Subject: "BrainBubblz: <nickname>'s week" for one child, "BrainBubblz: your children's week" for more, plus " (13–19 Nov)". HTML only: a short heading with the date range, one block per child (nickname as heading, the WE-7 lines as a list), a line "Open the parent area for the day-by-day view", and a footer: why they get it, "Turn off: Parent area → Account, or [turn off weekly emails](link)". Inline styles only, no images, no tracking pixels or tracked links. Every value is HTML-escaped (nicknames are parent text). Sender is the normal `noreply@brainbubblz.com`.
- **WE-10 One-click turn-off without signing in.** Hidden field `users.email_token` (text 32, `hidden: true`, filled with `$security.randomString(32)` the first time an email is built for that user). Link: `https://<host>/api/nn/weekly-email/off?u=<user id>&t=<token>`. `GET` shows a tiny HTML page with a "Turn off" button (link scanners pre-fetch GET, so GET alone must not change anything); the button `POST`s the same URL, which sets `weekly_email = false` when the token matches (constant-time compare) and answers "Weekly emails are off. You can turn them on again in the app." A wrong token answers the same page with "This link is not valid any more." The email also carries `List-Unsubscribe: <that URL>` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058), so mail apps' own unsubscribe button works. `mail.pb.js` passes `message.headers` on to Resend's `headers`. Rate limit rule `POST /api/nn/weekly-email/` 10/60 s (code-map gotcha: label with the method). — No new secret for Ken to set; a 32-character random token is unguessable and is never returned by the API.
- **WE-11 Admin run and preview.** `POST /api/nn/admin/weekly-email` (superuser only) `{now?: ISO, user?: id, preview?: bool}`: runs the same job as the cron with `now` instead of the clock, for one user or all; `preview: true` returns `{subject, html}` of that user's email for the week before `now`, sending and logging nothing (works even when the switch is off or the week has a row). Answer when not previewing: `{sent, skipped, failed, notDue}` counts. — Server tests drive time through it; Ken can preview a real email from the admin. The cron calls the same `lib/weekly.js` `run(app, nowMs, onlyUser)`.
- **WE-12 SCR-13 switch.** In the signed-in part of SCR-13, below the sync section: a `SwitchListTile` key `weekly-email`, "Weekly progress email", subtitle "Every Monday morning, a short summary of each child's week, sent to <email>." States: loading (switch off, disabled); with Plus and verified → enabled, shows the server value; turning it on writes `{weekly_email: true, weekly_email_tz}`, off writes `{weekly_email: false}`; a failed write puts the switch back and shows "Couldn't reach BrainBubblz. Try again when online." under it. Not verified → disabled, subtitle "Verify your email first to get weekly emails." No Plus → disabled, and a `PlusCard` (FEAT-30) under it. Offline when SCR-13 opens → disabled, "Connect to the internet to change this." The privacy note gains: "With the weekly email on, a summary of each child's practice is emailed to you each Monday."
- **WE-13 App data.** `ServerClient.weeklyEmail()` → `WeeklyEmailSetting {bool on, int tzMinutes}` (reads the own `users` record) and `ServerClient.setWeeklyEmail({required bool on, int? tzMinutes})` (updates it); both throw `SyncException` like the record calls. `FakeServerClient.weeklyEmailSetting` (settable), `weeklyEmailWrites`, `weeklyEmailFail`. Provider `weeklyEmailProvider` (autoDispose `FutureProvider`, invalidated after a write and on account change); the device offset comes through a `tzOffsetProvider` (default `DateTime.now().timeZoneOffset`, tests override; NFR-08). Nothing stored on the device.
- **WE-14 SCR-20 lists it.** "What Plus adds" gains "A weekly progress email" (PD-1 list in `plus_screen.dart`).

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1 (WEM-01, NFR-13)** Rules: a parent can read and change their own `weekly_email`/`weekly_email_tz` and not another's; `email_token` never appears in any API answer to a parent; `weekly_emails` is closed to parents (list, view, create, update, delete). Server `api_rules_test.dart` group `weekly_email`.
- **AC-2 (WEM-02)** With a Plus, verified, switched-on parent at tz 480 and seeded data: admin run at Monday 07:59 local sends nothing (`notDue`); at 08:00 sends one email (mail sink / Resend stub) to the account address and writes a `sent` row; a second run that week sends nothing; a run at Wednesday 08:00 local for a parent with no row sends nothing. A run where sending throws writes no row and the next run sends. Server `test/weekly_email_test.dart`.
- **AC-3 (WEM-02, WE-3)** No email for: switch off; unverified; no active Plus (expired entitlement); no child practice in the week (writes `skipped`). tz −300 parent gets the email at their own Monday 08:00, not Singapore's.
- **AC-4 (WEM-03)** Preview of seeded data shows, per child: time and days, rounds with revision count, questions and % right with the week before, topics to practise from `question_id` (including a mixed revision round), new badges by name, and "No practice this week." for an idle sibling; records outside the week (Sunday 23:59 local before, next Monday 00:00) are not counted; a nickname with `<b>` is escaped. `weekly_email_test.dart`.
- **AC-5 (WEM-05)** The sent email has the turn-off link and both List-Unsubscribe headers (Resend stub sees `headers`); GET the link changes nothing; POST with the right token turns the switch off; wrong token or user changes nothing; the rate limit answers 429 after 10 POSTs a minute. `weekly_email_test.dart`, a case in `resend_test.dart` for headers.
- **AC-6 (WEM-06)** Admin endpoint refuses a parent token (403) and anonymous calls; preview returns subject and html without sending or logging.
- **AC-7 (WE-8)** `test/tool/server_names_test.dart`: every topic in `topics.dart` and badge in `badges.dart` appears in `names.js` with the same name, and nothing extra.
- **AC-8 (WEM-01, WE-12/13)** Widget tests on SCR-13 (`test/features/parent/weekly_email_test.dart`): Plus + verified shows the server value; turning on writes `on` with the overridden tz offset; turning off writes off; a failed write restores the switch and shows the error line; unverified and no-Plus states are disabled (no-Plus shows the Plus card); offline is disabled; tz refresh on open when the offset changed; SCR-20 lists "A weekly progress email".
- **AC-9** `scripts/check.sh` green (format, analyze, app tests, server tests); CI green on the PR.
- **AC-10 (live, after the M9 deploy)** Ken's account with the switch on gets the Monday email; Ken previews it once with the admin call. Not blocking the merge (same as FEAT-29/36 live checks).
