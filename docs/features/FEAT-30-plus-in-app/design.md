# FEAT-30: Free tier and BrainBubblz Plus in the app

> Implements: PLS-01..08 (D24; plan §4, ADR-09, ADR-10 in `05-phase3-plan.md`) · Reads: ENT-03 (`GET /api/nn/entitlement`, FEAT-29 AD-5) · Touches: REV-03..05, PAR-06, PAR-09, PAR-11, FAM-01/02, SET-05, BNK-08/12/13 (now Plus or limited on free) · Screens: new SCR-20 Plus; SCR-02, SCR-05, SCR-09, SCR-10, SCR-11, SCR-18, SCR-19 (children step) change
> Status: built (T1–T7) · Branch: `feat/FEAT-30-plus-in-app` · 2026-11-20 (auto mode: decisions below taken by Claude on Ken's standing instruction)

## Scope

The app learns whether the family has Plus, keeps that answer offline for 7 days, and splits features into free and Plus as in plan §4. Parents see and manage Plus on a new SCR-20 inside the PIN-protected parent area (the parental gate both stores require). A child who meets a Plus feature sees one friendly lock that sends her to a grown-up, never a price or a buy button. Losing Plus hides Plus features and deletes nothing. Buying sits behind a `PurchaseService` interface whose only implementation now says "not available yet"; until FEAT-35 the only way to Plus is a complimentary grant (FEAT-29 AD-9/AD-10), which is how Ken's family and testers use and test it end to end.

Not in scope: store purchases, restore and manage (FEAT-35), invitation codes (FEAT-36 adds an "Enter a code" row to SCR-20), weekly email (FEAT-31), any server change (FEAT-29 is complete; its deploy goes with the M9 app release).

## Design decisions (this feature)

Auto mode (Ken, 2026-11-20): each takes the recommended option; the reason follows the dash.

- **PD-1 The split is plan §4, with these readings.** Plus: revision (REV-03 offers, REV-04 rounds, REV-05 weak types on SCR-10), PDF export (PAR-09), the day view (PAR-11), weekly trend charts on SCR-09 and SCR-10 (PAR-06), server bank updates (BNK-08/13), more than one child (FAM-01). Free: everything else, including the dashboard table and needs-practice list (PAR-05, PAR-07, PAR-08), topic locks, badges, screen time, account and sync. — §4 lists "progress reports: PDF, per-day view, trends" as Plus and "dashboard (accuracy and speed per topic)" as free; REV-05 is the revision module's output, so it goes with revision.
- **PD-2 Plus needs a signed-in account.** Entitlements belong to the parent account (ENT-01); a signed-out device is free. — One source of truth across devices and stores; no device-only Plus to migrate later. Consequence for Ken: every family tablet must be signed in after the M9 update to keep revision, PDF, day view and siblings (handoff, release note).
- **PD-3 Cached entitlement, one row on the device.** New Drift table `plus_state` (schema v12, single row `id = 1`, never synced): `plus` bool, `until` (UTC), `source`, `status`, `will_renew`, `ended_at`, `server_checked_at` (server's `checked_at`), `device_checked_at` (device clock at that moment), `account_id`. A table rather than `app_settings` columns: no `_from6To7` ordering trap, and it is device state, not a synced setting. Signing out, deleting the account or a different `account_id` signing in clears it.
- **PD-4 Plus is on (pure rule, `lib/domain/plus.dart`).** `plusActive(cache, now)` is true only if the cache exists and `plus` is true, and `now` is no earlier than `device_checked_at − 10 min` (a clock wound back before the last check ends the grace), and `now − device_checked_at ≤ 7 days` (offline grace, ADR-10), and `now + skew < until`, where `skew = server_checked_at − device_checked_at` (the server's clock judges the expiry, so a wrong tablet clock neither ends nor extends a store period early). The clock is a parameter (NFR-08). `plusFeature(PlusFeature f, bool active)` says whether a feature is open; the feature list lives in one enum, so a later split change is one line.
- **PD-5 When the app asks the server.** At launch when signed in, right after sign-in, after every successful sync run (SyncController already paces these), and on SCR-20's "Check again". A failed or offline check leaves the cache alone (the grace runs on); a 401 from a signed-out session clears it. — Reuses existing triggers; no new timer.
- **PD-6 Downgrade hides, never deletes.** Revision offers disappear; the day view, PDF and trends show a Plus card; banks already downloaded stay in use (BNK-13); with 2+ children only the **active child** plays, the others are kept, still sync (sync is free, so progress stays safe) and show "Paused – needs Plus" on SCR-18, where the parent can pick which child is active; "Who's playing?" (SCR-17) and the home child chip only show with Plus. The active child is the current child at the moment Plus ended. — Keeping all siblings playable on free would make "more than one child" free after one month; hiding them keeps their data intact and comes back the moment Plus does.
- **PD-7 Restoring or setting up a family on free.** SCR-19's children step allows 1 child on free ("More children come with Plus"), 4 with Plus. A sign-in that restores 2+ children from the server on a free account brings them all in (FAM-06, nothing lost) and applies PD-6. `ChildRepository.add` keeps its `maxChildren` check; the screen limit is `childLimit(plusActive)` (1 or 4).
- **PD-8 The child's lock is one card, gentle and rare.** On SCR-02, when a revision round would have been offered, a free child sees a "Revision" card with a padlock; tapping it shows a sheet: "Revision is part of BrainBubblz Plus. Ask a grown-up to have a look." with one "OK" button. No price, no store link, no parent-area link, no timer, not shown on SCR-05 or anywhere else. — The plan asks for "Ask a grown-up"; Google Play Families policy and Apple's Kids category forbid pushing children toward purchases, so the child gets one quiet signal in one place and no repeat nagging.
- **PD-9 SCR-20 Plus** (`/parent/plus`, route `Routes.plus`, from a "BrainBubblz Plus" row near the top of SCR-11 and from every parent-side Plus card). Shows: status line (free; "Plus until 31 Dec 2027"; complimentary FOREVER → "Plus, a gift from BrainBubblz"; store with `will_renew` → "renews on …", without → "ends on …"; `billing_issue` → "payment problem, check your store account"; grace running offline → "last checked 3 days ago"), what Plus adds (the PD-1 list), "Check again", and the buy area from `PurchaseService`. Signed out → "Sign in to use Plus" button to SCR-13. Parent-side Plus cards (SCR-09 day and trends, SCR-10 trends and weak types, SCR-11/SCR-09 PDF export, SCR-11 bank row "Updates with Plus", SCR-18 add child) are one shared widget `PlusCard` (`lib/ui/plus_card.dart`) that opens SCR-20. The parent area is behind the PIN, so these can mention buying; nothing outside it does.
- **PD-10 `PurchaseService` interface** (`lib/data/purchase_service.dart`): `bool get available`, `Future<List<PlusOffer>> offers()`, `Future<PurchaseOutcome> buy(PlusOffer)`, `Future<void> restore()`, `Uri? get manageUrl`. Only implementation now: `UnavailablePurchaseService` (`available = false`); SCR-20 then shows "Buying Plus in the app is coming soon." and no button. `purchaseServiceProvider` in `providers.dart`; FEAT-35 swaps in RevenueCat (ADR-09) and calls the entitlement refresh after a buy. — The screen and tests are built against the interface now, so FEAT-35 only adds an implementation.
- **PD-11 Server client.** `ServerClient.entitlement()` → `Entitlement` (data class mirroring AD-5) from `GET /api/nn/entitlement`; `FakeServerClient` gets a settable `entitlement`. Bank: `BankUpdater.check` returns "no Plus" without calling the server when Plus is off (saves a call the server would answer with an empty list); SCR-11's bank row says "Question updates come with Plus".
- **PD-12 Debug and tests.** No hidden switch in release builds. Widget tests set Plus through `plusProvider` overrides (`QuizHarness`/`ParentHarness` default `plus: true`, so the existing tests keep their behaviour; new free-tier tests pass `plus: false`).

## Design

- **Domain** `lib/domain/plus.dart`: `PlusCache`, `plusActive(cache, now)`, `PlusFeature` enum (`revision`, `pdfReport`, `dayView`, `trends`, `bankUpdates`, `siblings`), `childLimit(bool plus)`, `plusStatusLine(cache, now)` (SCR-20 wording, pure so it is unit-tested).
- **Data** `database.dart` v12 `PlusState` table + migration test; `lib/data/plus_repository.dart` (`PlusRepository`: `cached()`, `refresh()` via `ServerClient.entitlement()`, `clear()`); `Entitlement` + `entitlement()` in `server_client.dart`; `purchase_service.dart` (PD-10). Providers: `plusRepositoryProvider`, `plusCacheProvider` (FutureProvider, invalidated after refresh/clear), `plusProvider` (bool, `plusActive(cache, clock.now())`), `purchaseServiceProvider`. Hooks: `AccountRepository` sign-in/out/delete, `SyncController` after a successful run, app start (PD-5).
- **Gates**: `revisionOffersProvider` false and `/revise/*` redirects home when free; home revision lock card (PD-8); dashboard day card, trends, PDF button; topic detail trends and weak types; SCR-11 bank row and Plus row; SCR-18 add child and paused children with "Make active"; SCR-17 picker and home chip (`pickerShows` also needs Plus); SCR-19 children step limit; `BankUpdater.check` (PD-11).
- **Screen** `lib/features/parent/plus_screen.dart` (SCR-20), `lib/ui/plus_card.dart`. Texts in plain English (copy in PD-8, PD-9). Phone layout through `compact(size)` like other parent screens.
- **Docs**: `02-design.md` §6 SCR-20 row and change log line; `01-requirements.md` D24 + PLS rows (done in this design); code-map lines (build).

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** (PLS-02, PD-4): unit tests of `plusActive`: no cache → false; plus with `until` ahead and checked now → true; 7 days + 1 min since the check → false; clock wound back 11 min before the check → false; tablet clock 2 h slow with the server's `until` 1 h away → false at server time; `plus false` → false.
- **AC-2** (PLS-01, PD-3, PD-5): `PlusRepository` with `FakeServerClient`: refresh stores the entitlement; an offline refresh keeps the old cache; a 401 clears it; sign-out and account delete clear it; signing in as another account replaces it. Migration test v11 → v12 keeps every other table.
- **AC-3** (PLS-03, PLS-04, PD-1, PD-6): widget tests on free: no revision offer on SCR-02 or SCR-05 and `/revise/mixed` goes home; SCR-09 shows Plus cards instead of the day card and trends, and no PDF export; SCR-10 hides trends and weak types; SCR-11 bank row says updates come with Plus. With Plus each shows as today (existing tests, default `plus: true`).
- **AC-4** (PLS-05, PD-6, PD-7): free with 3 children: only the active child plays, no "Who's playing?", no home chip; SCR-18 lists the others as paused with "Make active", which switches the active child; "Add child" opens SCR-20; nothing is deleted from `children` or progress tables; turning Plus back on shows the picker again. SCR-19 children step allows one child on free and four with Plus.
- **AC-5** (PLS-06, PD-8): free child on SCR-02 with revision due sees the lock card; tapping shows the "Ask a grown-up" sheet with one OK and no price, store or parent link; SCR-05 shows no lock.
- **AC-6** (PLS-07, PD-9): SCR-20 status lines for: signed out (Sign in button opens SCR-13), free, complimentary FOREVER, store renewing, store ending, billing issue, offline grace; "Check again" refreshes; reached from SCR-11 and from a `PlusCard`; only inside the PIN area.
- **AC-7** (PLS-08, PD-10): with `UnavailablePurchaseService` SCR-20 shows "coming soon" and no buy button; with a fake available service it lists the offer and `buy` is called (proves the interface FEAT-35 fills).
- **AC-8** (PD-11, BNK-13): `BankUpdater.check` on free makes no server call and keeps the current bank; with Plus it checks as before.
- **AC-9** (live, with the M9 release, not needed to merge): Ken's signed-in tablet on the deployed FEAT-29 server shows "Plus, a gift from BrainBubblz" and keeps revision, PDF, day view and both children.
